# Respirator

A mask worn in the Curios **Face** slot. With a filter cartridge it concentrates what oxygen thin air still has and cuts fume poison to a fifth; with an air cylinder it breathes its own air anywhere.

## Crafting

Made like any [modular tool](Tools_and_Weapons.md), from three parts laid out in a column on the [Blueprint Table](Blueprint_Table.md) drawing: face piece, strap, then one **supply**. The supply decides the kind of mask.

- **Respirator Face Piece**: the part that seals over the nose and mouth, shaped from soft sheet such as leather or cloth.
- **Strap**: leather, string or wire.
- **Filter Cartridge** (supply): a cartridge of porous media such as charcoal. Its **bed** stepper (1 to 10 cm, 4 by default) is the depth of the packing.
- **Air Cylinder** (supply): a cylinder shaped from plate. Its **wall** stepper (1 to 10 mm, 3 by default) is the wall thickness.

## Using it

Wear it in the Face slot. It acts by itself whenever you would otherwise suffer from [thin air](Hazards.md#thin-air) or a fume dose; there is nothing to switch on.

### Filter

- **Thin air.** The filter works down to a floor of thin air. A deeper bed reaches thinner air and lasts longer. Below the floor it gives nothing.
- **Fumes.** Any fume dose, whether from a cloud, a boiling melt, an anode effect or molten leaded solder, gives a fifth of the poison it would. It stacks with the [Fume ward](Skills.md) skill: both cuts apply together.
- **Wear.** It wears about a point a second while it supplies air, and breaks when worn out.
- **Breathing.** A deeper bed makes breathing harder: it slows your sprint more.

### Air cylinder

The cylinder holds air and draws on it while the air outside is too thin to breathe. A thicker wall of a stronger metal holds more. The [Gas Compressor](Gas_Compressor.md) fills it; the air it holds shows on its tooltip like a fluid in any container. It works at any height and in the [Shelf](The_Shelf.md). An empty cylinder gives nothing.
