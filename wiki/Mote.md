# Mote

One voxel of a block: a 64th of it, a quarter of a block on a side. Motes are what you build with in [fine space](Fine_Space.md).

## Obtaining

Cut a whole block into 64 motes at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [Sawblade](Sawblade.md) in a tool slot. No fasteners are needed, and it needs Mechanical Treatise I: Engines, Flywheels and Dynamotors ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

```
B
```

B is any plain, solid, whole block: stone, planks, a metal block, wool. A block with a menu or machinery, a half block, a fluid or anything that is not a full cube cannot be cut. The motes are named for their block ("Stone Mote") and motes of one block stack. Mining a voxel with the right tool gives its mote back.

## Using it

Right-click any block or any piece of [fine space](Fine_Space.md) with a mote in hand. It goes in the voxel in front of the face you clicked, at the exact spot you pointed. Where there was only air, the click makes the fine space too. While you hold a mote, an outline shows the voxel it will fill.

Blocks place the way they do full size: a log lies along its axis, a stair takes the half you clicked. Each voxel is drawn as its block's own model at a quarter of the size, and you walk on and bump into the exact voxels. A glowing block lights the space around it.

Parts that work in fine space ([shafts](Shaft.md), [cogs](Cogs.md), [joints](Vehicles.md#joints), [bearings](Bearing.md)) are placed as themselves, not cut into motes. So are [modular machines](Fine_Space.md#machines-in-fine-space), clicked onto a cell that already exists.

A [Fine Gauge](Fine_Gauge.md) held in either hand sets how motes go in: one at a time, a line, a box, mirrored, or as a ramp. A [Fine Wedge](Fine_Wedge.md) is a ready-made ramp voxel.
