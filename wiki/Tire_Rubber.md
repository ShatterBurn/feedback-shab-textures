# Tire Rubber

A block of pressed [synthetic rubber](Materials.md#polymers): a soft, grippy building block that collides like any other. It is not a wheel; a wheel is a [Tire Hub](Tire_Hub.md) fitted with a [tire](Tire.md).

## Crafting

```
RR
RR
```

R is a Synthetic Rubber Ingot. The recipe makes 1.

## Using it

Place it as a block, or cut it into [motes](Mote.md) and place them in [fine space](Fine_Space.md). Rubber has a friction of 1.2, so it grips more than most blocks.
