# Piton

A spike with an eye, driven into the face of a block. Two pitons joined with a [Grapple](Grapple.md) carry a fixed line you can hang from and climb.

## Crafting

```
P P
_ R
_ R
```

P is a Wrought Iron Plate and R a Wrought Iron Rod. Makes 2 pitons.

## Using it

Place it against the face of a block. It has no collision, so you walk through it. Tie a line between two pitons with a grapple, climb it, and cut it; see [Fixed lines](Grapple.md#fixed-lines).

## How it works

A piton holds only as well as the block it is driven into. Stone, logs and metal hold a person easily; soft blocks such as dirt work loose under a load and the piton gives way (see [What the hook bites into](Grapple.md#what-the-hook-bites-into)). The line itself is rated by its material ([The line has a rating](Grapple.md#the-line-has-a-rating)).

Breaking a piton cuts its line.
