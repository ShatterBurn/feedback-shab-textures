# Research Table

The Research Table is where what is not yet known is found out. It writes a [Blank Treatise](Treatise.md#blank-treatise) into a treatise volume, or teaches you an attunement you have not learned, by working out, on a board of linked concepts, how something you already have leads to the new idea.

## Crafting

```
P P P
W L W
W W W
```

P is paper, W is any plank, L is a lectern.

## Using it

The screen is an open book. The left page is a list with two tabs, **Treatises** and **Attunements**; each entry has a spinning icon. Click an entry to pick it. The right page shows the picked entry's two-line blurb, what it asks for, its slots and the **Research** button, which opens the research board and costs 40 experience points.

A volume has a slot for one Blank Treatise and a slot for one **specimen**. An attunement has a slot for an **offering** and a slot for a **reagent**. The text on the right page says what is missing.

### Specimens

A **first volume** takes a plain thing:

| Volume | Specimen |
| --- | --- |
| Weapons I | sword, knife, axe or hafted tool |
| Mechanical I | hand crank or water wheel |
| Aerospace I | water wheel |
| Robotics I | dynamotor or electromagnet |
| Chemical I | crucible or thermal store |
| Materials I | hand hammer or file |

A volume after the first takes an item built from the volume before it, and that earlier treatise must be held: in your pack, or on a [Treatise Shelf](Treatise_Binder_and_Shelf.md) beside the table.

| Volume | Specimen |
| --- | --- |
| Weapons II | cleaver, maul or spear |
| Weapons III | crossbow, gun or spring gun |
| Mechanical II | engine or flywheel |
| Aerospace II | propeller |
| Chemical II | boiler, gas tank or pressure vessel |
| Chemical III | compressor or heat exchanger |

### Attunements

Each attunement is about one material property: hardness, toughness, strength, weight, heat resistance, conduction or heat capacity. Its power sets a bar. The **offering** is any stock of a material, and it must rank at least that far up the property among all materials; the screen calls the bar modest, good, fine or exceptional. The further the offering is over the bar, the more ink it gives, up to 4 extra. **Mend** is about toughness and asks for an offering in the top tenth of materials by toughness; a vanilla mending book teaches it too (see [Knowing an effect](Enchanting_Table.md#knowing-an-effect)).

The **reagent** is a brewed potion.

Some attunements, Heat and Ward among them, name a potion. A brewed potion carrying the named effect, still potent, qualifies by itself and gives 3 ink, which makes the board easier. If the offering is not enough, the screen says it needs an offering that is good enough, or the potion it names.

One offering is used up per run, and the potion with it. No specimen is worn.

## The research board

Each volume has its own story and its own concepts. Attunements share one story, "Marking the unseen".

The board is a hex board. The concept you already know sits at one end (the start) and the new idea at the other (the goal). Six hexes are rubble and cannot be used. Beside the board is a hand of concepts.

Every concept shows two or three **traits** under its name, such as force, thin, hard or pressure. Two concepts link when they share a trait. Hover a concept on the board, or the goal, to see its traits.

1. Pick a concept from the hand. The side list names which concepts on the board it shares traits with.
2. Hover a free hex beside the chain. The chain concepts it would link with are outlined in green and the line names the shared trait; if it would share nothing, the line says so. Hovering costs nothing.
3. Click the hex. If the concept shares a trait with a chain concept it touches, it joins the chain with a rising note. If not, it fizzles, naming the concepts the hex touched, costs a point of **ink** and, on a volume, **strains** the specimen.
4. Three joins in a row give an ink back.
5. To finish, place a concept on a hex beside the goal that shares one of the goal's traits. A line under the ink pips says what the goal needs. The finding is then replayed line by line and asks one question, and a stamp grades the notes.

A three-step guide shows over the board the first time you open it in a game session; click to dismiss it.

The notes' quality comes from the ink left, the longest run of joins and the answer to the question. Running out of ink first leaves a scrawl.

### Ink

A run starts with seven ink. A volume adds up to three more from a specimen in good condition; an attunement adds ink from its offering and potion (see [Attunements](#attunements)). A bonus is added if you have already done the real thing a good number of times: fired a crossbow many times before researching guns, or forged with a hammer before Materials I.

### Strain

On a volume, a few fizzles wear the specimen: it comes back worn and can be repaired. Many fizzles take it apart into its parts, which go to you.

## What comes out

A treatise carries the quality as its notes. An attunement is simply learned. The experience is spent either way, and a scrawl still turns the Blank Treatise into the volume. Pressing Esc before closing the notebook spends nothing.
