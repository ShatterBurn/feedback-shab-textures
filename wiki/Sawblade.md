# Sawblade

A bare blade, with no handle, that saws logs and planks in your hands and goes in the [Stonecutter](Stonecutter.md)'s saw slot. The stonecutter does nothing without one.

## Crafting

Cast a **Saw Blade** in a [Saw Blade Mold](Casting.md#tool-part-molds) from one ingot's worth of any metal the mold takes, or shape one from a metal plate, then put it together in a crafting grid as described in [Tools and Weapons](Tools_and_Weapons.md#putting-one-together). A sawblade also goes in a tool slot of the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) as its saw. The blade's material sets how long the sawblade lasts.

## Sawing in the world

Hold the sawblade and look at a log or a plank block within reach, then **hold right-click**. A stroke lands about every third of a second, wearing the blade one point and making you a little hungrier each time. Looking away from the block, or letting go, stops the cut and the block stays as it was.

A stroke takes off more the stronger your arm is and the thinner the blade is, and a harder wood takes more strokes. **The blade must be harder than the wood.** A softer blade cuts nothing.

When the cut is done the block is gone and drops its sawn form: a log gives 4 planks of its species, and a plank block gives slabs. The kerf falls too, as that wood's shavings ([Materials](Materials.md#woods)). A thinner blade wastes less wood, so it drops fewer shavings.

## Using it at a bench

Put the sawblade in the saw slot of the Stonecutter. Every act there costs it a point of wear: shaping a part, breaking stock down, or a cut on the Cut tab (stone cuts, and wood cuts when a plank or log is in the center). With the slot empty the screen reads "Put a sawblade in the saw slot."

The [Woodworking Table](Woodworking_Table.md) also takes a sawblade in its tool slot. The blade has a **thickness** setting at the part bench: a thicker blade sheds more of each cut as wood shavings.
