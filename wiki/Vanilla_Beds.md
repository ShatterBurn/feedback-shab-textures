# Vanilla Beds

Vanilla's sixteen colored beds cannot be crafted or dyed. You build a [Bed](Bed.md) from a mattress and a frame, and beds found in the world are Feedback beds.

## What vanilla does

A bed is three wool of one color over three planks in a crafting grid. A bed can be recolored by crafting it with a dye. A placed bed has two blocks, sleeps one player, skips the night, sets your respawn point, and explodes if you try to sleep in the Nether or the End. Villagers claim a bed as their home and sleep in it.

## What Feedback does

No vanilla bed recipe exists, dyeing included, and the vanilla beds are hidden from JEI. The beds you make come from a **Bed Mattress** part and a **Bed Frame** part, shaped at the benches and joined in a crafting grid (see [Bed](Bed.md#crafting)).

A placed Feedback bed does everything a vanilla bed does:

- It sleeps you, skips the night and sets your respawn point.
- It explodes if you sleep in it in the Nether or the End.
- Villagers sleep in it and claim it as their home. A villager claims the head half; the foot half is not claimed.

## Beds found in the world

Any vanilla bed that turns up in a world, village or structure, or that is placed by a command, becomes a Feedback bed a moment later. Both halves change together and keep their facing. Each bed is drawn at random, and the same bed always draws the same way:

- **Frame:** mostly wood (oak, spruce, birch, dark oak, jungle or acacia), sometimes bronze or wrought iron. Most are bedsteads; some are cots.
- **Mattress:** a flat mattress with a cover of wool, coated cloth or leather, over padding of wool, feather down, short grass, tall grass or rabbit hide.
- **Color:** the cover keeps the color the vanilla bed had.

A placed bed cannot be dyed. Take it up and rework its parts to change the cover.

Breaking a found bed returns it as one item with its parts, as with any placed bed.
