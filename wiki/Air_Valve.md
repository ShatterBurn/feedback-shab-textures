# Air Valve

A wall valve that lets air from a [pipe](Pipe.md) into the sealed cabin it opens on, topping up the air people breathe there.

## Crafting

```
S V S
K
```

S is a Wrought Iron Plate, V is a [Valve](Valve.md) and K is a [Coil Spring](Lathe.md#what-it-makes) of any metal. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 2 screws ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)). It needs Mechanical Treatise I: Engines, Flywheels and Dynamotors ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

Set it in the hull with a pipe of air on one side and the cabin on the other. A compressed-air bucket also feeds it. About once a second it moves air from the pipe into the cabin on any open side.

## The cabin

A **cabin** is the open volume a hull closes inside a craft. It holds air, and people breathe it down; a hole in the hull lets it leak out, faster than a valve can supply, and a cabin with a hole loses air while nobody is in it too: the next person in finds what it bled. While a cabin holds air you can breathe in it where the air outside is too thin (see [Hazards](Hazards.md#thin-air)), including in the [Gap](The_Gap.md).
