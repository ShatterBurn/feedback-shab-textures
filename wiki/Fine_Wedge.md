# Fine Wedge

A four-step ramp for [fine space](Fine_Space.md). As a voxel it is a ramp a quarter of a block long, falling away from you.

## Crafting

```
S 
SS
```

S is a Smooth Stone Slab. The recipe makes 2.

## Using it

Hold it and right-click a block or a piece of fine space as you would place a [mote](Mote.md). The ramp falls away from you. Click the upper half of a face to hang it from above instead.

Each of the four steps is 1/16 of a block. What you see is what collides and what the outline shows: the same four steps. The wedge has a single look.

## Wedge mode

Set a [Fine Gauge](Fine_Gauge.md) to **Wedge** mode, hold it in one hand and a mote in the other, and the mote goes in as a ramp of itself. It takes each face's own texture and tint (a grass wedge is green on top), and mining it gives the mote back. Rotation parts and joints cannot be cut to a wedge.
