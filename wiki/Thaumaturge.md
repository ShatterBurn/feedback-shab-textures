# Thaumaturge

With the **Thaumaturge** mod installed (a Thaumcraft port with aspects and a Thaumometer for scanning), Feedback items carry Thaumaturge aspects, so scanning them works as it does for any other item. Without Thaumaturge, nothing changes.

## Materials

Every form of a material (ingot, dust, block, plate, nugget and the rest) carries aspects worked out from what the material is and how it behaves:

| Property of the material | Aspect |
|---|---|
| Metal | Metal |
| Mineral | Earth |
| Glass and gems | Crystal |
| Fluids | Water or Air |
| Plants | Plant |
| Animals | Beast and Life |
| Electrical conductivity | Energy |
| High reflectivity | Light |
| Flammable or burning | Fire |
| Toxicity | Death |
| Hardness and alloys | Order |
| Magnetism | Motion |
| Powders | Entropy |

Amounts follow the size of the form: a nugget carries less than an ingot, a block more. They also follow the material's figures, so a denser metal carries more Metal and a better conductor more Energy. See [Materials](Materials.md) for the properties themselves.

## Machines and devices

Machines and devices carry aspects that match their function:

- Mechanisms: Mechanism and Motion.
- Tools and workshop machines: Tool and Craft.
- Heat machines: Fire. Cold machines: Ice. See [Heat](Heat.md).
- Electrical parts: Energy. See [Electricity](Electricity.md).
- Sensors: Senses.
- Controllers and computers: Mind. See [Controller](Controller.md).
- Experience, ender and sculk gear: Magic. See [Experience](Experience.md) and [Sculk](Sculk.md).
- Reactor parts: their own aspects, set by what they do. See [Fission Reactor](Fission_Reactor.md).

Part items that carry their material on the stack, and creative-only blocks, have no aspects of their own. Thaumaturge works theirs out from their recipes.
