# Locked Joint

A joint, in ordinary blocks or [finespace](Finespace.md), that holds two bodies together as one rigid piece. It splits a build into parts that can be put back separately, or anchors a build to a block in the world.

## Crafting

```
I B I
R
```

I is a Wrought Iron Plate, B is a [Rotary Bearing](Rotary_Bearing.md) and R is a Wrought Iron Rod. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 2 screws ([Fasteners](Fasteners.md)). It needs Mechanical Treatise I: Engines, Flywheels and Dynamotors ([Treatise](Treatise.md)).

## Using it

It works as a full-size block and as a voxel of [finespace](Finespace.md).

1. Place it against a block, or put it in finespace, with its front toward the part to split off.
2. **Use it with an empty hand** to assemble what is joined to its front face into a second body, held rigidly to the first.
3. **Use it again** to put the body back, at the nearest of the 24 ways a cube can be turned.

Placed against a world block, it anchors the build to that block.
