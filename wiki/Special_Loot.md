# Special Loot

Items that turn up as finds in chests and are also crafted at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md). Each section says where the item is found and how it is crafted. The three below are bracer-slot [combat gear](Combat_Gear.md): you wear one of the three.

## Feedbacker

A gauntlet that **punches any projectile back** at whoever sent it, faster than it came. The name is borrowed from the [Feedbacker](https://ultrakill.fandom.com/wiki/Feedbacker) arm in ULTRAKILL, whose parry it echoes; it is not named after this mod.

It is a bracer-slot [combat gear](Combat_Gear.md) piece, so it shares its slot with the [Knuckleblaster](#knuckleblaster) and the [Whiplash](#whiplash). It grants the technique itself: no skill needed.

### Where to find it and how to craft it

```
P C P
P E P
  L
```

P is a Steel Plate, C is a [Heating Coil](Heating_Coil.md) (any), E is an [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) and L is Leather. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 3 [fasteners](Fasteners.md). It needs Electrical Treatise III: Circuits ([Treatise](Treatise.md)). The gauntlet comes out tempered steel.

It is also a **rare find** (about 4%) in dungeon, stronghold and ancient city chests, already 30 to 70% worn.

### Using it

Wear it in the bracer slot, and press **G** (parry) with **nothing in your hand**. A window opens (5 ticks, a 75° cone, 20 ticks of recovery).

- **Any projectile** (arrow, bolt, thrown weapon) that reaches you in the window is turned and sent back along your look at **2.5×** their speed.
- **A ghast fireball** comes back as well, without exploding on you. It costs much more of the gauntlet.
- **Bullets** from a [gun](Guns.md): if you punch as a shot is fired, facing the shooter, it is negated and a return shot hits the shooter for **2.5× the bullet's damage**, credited to you. A punched-back shot can't be parried again. (A *weapon* parry doesn't catch bullets: they land.)

It plays its own sound, and the arm you punch with is drawn with the gauntlet's skin, in first and third person, for you and anyone watching.

### What it costs

A punch takes **1 wear plus 12 for every whole multiple of the gauntlet's rating** (150 impulse, raised by the worn gear's joint share) the projectile carried. An arrow is about 45; a ghast fireball is 120 to 150; anything much heavier is far over. A punch that is over the rating wrecks it for 150 wear.

It has 400 durability, and **it is never destroyed**: at its last point it simply stops granting the punch. It cannot be repaired.

## Knuckleblaster

A gauntlet for **heavy punches and a shockwave**: one slow blow that hits hard and breaks glass, and a burst that shoves creatures and turns projectiles away from you.

It is a bracer-slot [combat gear](Combat_Gear.md) piece, so it shares its slot with the [Feedbacker](#feedbacker) and the [Whiplash](#whiplash). It grants its techniques itself; no skill needed.

### Where to find it and how to craft it

```
P P P
C E C
  L
```

P is a Steel Plate, C is a [Heating Coil](Heating_Coil.md) (any), E is an [Electromagnet](Electromagnet.md) and L is Leather. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 3 [fasteners](Fasteners.md). It needs Weapons Treatise I: Blades and Heads ([Treatise](Treatise.md)). The recipe appears once you have unlocked the tempered steel plate. The gauntlet comes out tempered steel.

It is also a **rare find** (about 3%) in dungeon, stronghold and ancient city chests, already 30 to 70% worn.

### Using it

Wear it in the bracer slot and press **G** (the parry key) with **nothing in your hand**.

- **Heavy punch.** A slow blow that deals **6 damage** and knocks the target back hard: the lighter the target, the farther it flies. It also breaks glass and glass panes in reach. It costs stamina and wears the gauntlet.
- **Shockwave.** Click for the punch, then keep the key held for a full second after it and the gauntlet fires a burst ahead of you, in a radius of **3.5 blocks**. Creatures in it take **1 damage** and are shoved away; you are unharmed. Projectiles in it are turned along your look at **1.5×** their speed. The burst costs more stamina and wear.

There is no parry and no healing. The left arm is drawn with a red and black gauntlet skin, in first and third person, for you and anyone watching.

### What it costs

Each punch and each shockwave takes stamina and wears the gauntlet. At its last point it stops working.

### Punch exhaustion

A rested arm can punch again after about **0.4 seconds**. Each punch thrown before the arm has recovered adds about 0.2 seconds to the wait, up to about **1.2 seconds**, so a long string of punches slows down. Once the arm has been idle for a moment, the next punch is quick again.

After a shockwave the arm is spent for about **1.9 seconds**.

## Whiplash

An arm that throws a **barbed spear on a lanyard** from your wrist, so both hands stay free for a sword or a gun. One press throws it, and the line reels at once.

It is a bracer-slot [combat gear](Combat_Gear.md) piece, so it shares its slot with the [Feedbacker](#feedbacker) and the [Knuckleblaster](#knuckleblaster). It grants its technique itself; no skill needed.

### How to craft it

```
P R P
C W C
  L
```

P is a Wrought Iron Plate, R a Wrought Iron Rod, C a Chain, W a Grapple Winch (see [Grapple](Grapple.md#crafting)) and L Leather. Craft it at the [Engineering Table](Engineering_Table.md) with a [screwdriver](Hand_Tools.md#screwdriver) in the tool row and 3 [fasteners](Fasteners.md). It needs Weapons Treatise I: Blades and Heads ([Treatise](Treatise.md)). The recipe appears once you have unlocked the wrought iron plate. The arm comes out wrought iron.

### Using it

Wear it in the bracer slot. Press **B** (*Whiplash*, rebindable) once to throw the spear along your look. It lands where it likes (a block, a creature, an item or a projectile) and the line reels at once, with no holding or releasing. The reel ends when you and the spear are together.

- **A creature up to three times your mass** (by body size, the same stand-in as the [parry](Combat.md#parry)'s) is dragged to you, slower the heavier it is than you.
- **A heavier creature or a block** pulls you to it, at a steady speed on the ground as in the air.
- **A creature** is bitten for **1 damage**.
- **A creature reeled in** keeps the spear in it, holding it, and the spear pulls again if it breaks away. It stays until you press the key again, sneak, or the creature dies.
- **Press the key again with a spear out** to retrieve that spear, also while it is still flying. A further press throws another.
- **Jump, or punch with a [Knuckleblaster](#knuckleblaster),** to cancel the pull and keep your momentum.

The spear also takes what it touches:

- A **dropped item** is fetched to your feet.
- A **projectile in flight** is held where it hangs, then drawn to you and sent along your look at **1.5 times** its speed, like a parry.
- A **coin** of yours in the spear's path is knocked back up (see Coin flip under [Skills](Skills.md#observation)).

### The line

The line is the arm's strap, wrought iron, so its rating follows wrought iron's yield strength as a [grapple](Grapple.md#the-line-has-a-rating) line's does, and it parts, strains and burns the same way.

### What it costs

Each throw takes **6 stamina**, wears the arm 1 point and has a short cooldown. At its last point the arm stops working.

The left arm is drawn with a green skin, in first and third person, for you and anyone watching.
