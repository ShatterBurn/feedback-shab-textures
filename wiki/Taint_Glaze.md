# Taint Glaze

A pot of glaze brushed onto one placed block. A glazed machine does not corrode in a chunk flooded with Thaumaturge's flux (see [Mod Compats](Mod_Compats.md#flux-scrubbing-and-rust)).

## Crafting

Glazed Ceramic + 2 Clay Balls (shapeless) makes 2 Taint Glaze.

## Using it

**Right-click a placed block** with the glaze to brush it. One block takes one glaze. A block that is already glazed refuses it and the glaze is kept. The glaze belongs to that block: if the block is broken or replaced, what stands there is bare.

## How it works

Without Thaumaturge, flux never builds up and the glaze changes nothing. With it, bare [shafts](Shaft.md), [pulleys](Pulleys.md), [dynamotors](Dynamotor.md), [converters](Converter.md), [heating coils](Heating_Coil.md), [relays](Relay.md), [electromagnets](Electromagnet.md) and [batteries](Battery.md) in a flooded chunk slowly lose condition; a glazed one does not. A [Scrubbing Bed](Scrubbing_Bed.md) that brings the flux down protects every machine in the chunk at once, where a glaze protects one.
